Days 0–30 Learn Fast, Touch Everyone, Protect Run-Rate
Get plugged into the team, touch every account, and make it safe to keep spending with Dell.

Objectives

  • Align with leadership on what “good” looks like for this role and territory.
  • Touch every account at least once (preferably onsite for current spenders).
  • Sell meetings and conversations, not products, in early outreach.
  • Proactively acknowledge recent churn risk (four AEs in the last year) to rebuild trust.
Mindset for the first 30 days ▾
  • Expect some mess and missing information; don’t let it slow activity.
  • Keep activity high even if immediate response is low.
  • Treat every call or meeting like a rep in the gym to build resilience and skill.

High-Velocity Outreach

  • Goal: touch every account in the first month.
  • Use all channels: current contacts, phone, email, and onsite “donut drop-offs.”
  • With current spenders:
    • Thank them for the existing partnership.
    • Emphasize protecting their investments and showing smart next steps (not random pitches).
    • Show genuine interest in their long-term goals and Dell partnership.
Early themes to plant ▾
  • Dell & AI: position Dell as a partner for AI-ready infrastructure and AI-Ops.
  • Security & Services: data protection, resilience, and managed services.
  • Messaging example: “Most of my customers are trying to secure data and simplify operations. I’d love to hear where you are on that.”
  • Connect Dell strengths to healthcare and higher ed patterns and regulations.

Training & Internal Alignment

  • Schedule time with Kerrigan, Mick, Craig, Brad, Shannon, and Manny to learn:
    • How they prioritize accounts and time.
    • Examples of what “good” looks like in this territory.
    • Common traps in healthcare and higher ed.
  • Agree on a weekly 15–30 minute check-in and 1–2 skills each will coach me on.

MyService360 / AIOps Motion

  • Build the operational heartbeat for the territory using MyService360 / AIOps to:
    • See asset concentration by account.
    • Pull contact info and locations.
    • Build a rough asset overview to steer conversations.

Segment Accounts & Focus on Run-Rate

  • Break the territory into tiers:
    • Tier A: current spenders (largest revenue, AMO, or active projects).
    • Tier B: dormant (past spend, no recent orders).
    • Tier C: small potential or early-stage relationships.
  • For Tier A immediately:
    • Work with Shannon in MyService360 to see what is installed.
    • Identify capacity/refresh risks, support/performance issues, expiring contracts, and EOS gear.

Risk View: Red / Yellow / Green

  • Create a simple account risk view:
    • Red: high churn risk.
    • Yellow: neutral / at risk if ignored.
    • Green: advocates / referenceable.
  • Use this view to prioritize outreach and response times.
Days 31–60 Keep My Word, Tell the Story, Build the Pipeline
Deepen discovery, co-create success with key accounts, and stand up a clear pipeline & forecast.

Objectives

  • Co-create with key accounts what “good” looks like and how we’ll measure it.
  • Start telling a consistent Dell story across AI, security, and services.
  • Build a clear pipeline / forecast view for the rest of the year (by ~Day 45).
  • Use real patterns (e.g., Vanderbilt) to paint the “art of the possible.”

Deeper Discovery with Tier A Accounts

  • For each Tier A account:
    • Book 60–90 minute working sessions with IT + at least one business or clinical stakeholder.
    • In those sessions, cover:
      • Where they are now: assets, services, pain points (anchored in MyService360 data).
      • What success looks like for clinicians, staff, or students.
      • Constraints: budget, timing, risk tolerance.
Get out of IT and see the work ▾
  • Ask IT for:
    • Hospital walk-throughs and time with clinicians/front-line staff.
    • In higher ed, time with faculty or department admins.
  • Use observations to ground proposals in real workflows and outcomes.

Start Telling the Dell Story

  • Shift from pure discovery to a clear, outcome-based value narrative.
  • AI:
    • AI-ready infrastructure and AI-Ops that reduce firefighting.
    • Frame AI in terms of specific use cases they care about (e.g., clinician experience, student success).
  • Security & Services:
    • Zero-trust-aligned infrastructure and data protection / cyber recovery.
    • Managed services that take operational work off their plate.
  • Driving human progress: connect Dell’s mission to their mission so the story centers on their impact.

TnB / Seeds – Keep Them Evaluating

  • Reinforce that it’s healthy for them to keep evaluating options.
  • Position Dell as a partner in that evaluation, not just a vendor.
  • Offer:
    • Try & Buy (TnB) and hardware/software seeds to de-risk decisions.
    • A small backup or cyber recovery pilot.
    • A performance or health assessment.
    • A limited-scope infrastructure pilot for one clinic, department, or workload.

Around Day 45: Build the Pipeline Picture

  • Build a simple view of pipeline for the rest of the year by:
    • Account.
    • Deal size.
    • Tower / solution area.
    • Stage and probability.
  • Review pipeline with Kerrigan, Mick, and Manny and get feedback on where to focus.
  • Use the view with SEs and specialists to direct effort to the right places.
Days 61–90 Anchor Wins, Bigger Vision, Expansion
Convert pilots into anchor wins, build Now/Next/Later roadmaps, and cement plans to grow spend.

Objectives

  • Turn one or two key opportunities into real deals that serve as anchor wins.
  • Show each account a realistic Now / Next / Later story.
  • Lock in plans to keep current spenders both spending and growing.
  • Show visible progress and resilience in activity, pipeline, and wins.

Turn Pilots into Wins

  • For every pilot or TnB:
    • Capture results and feedback quickly.
    • Turn results into a proposal and brief value summary.
  • Prioritize current spenders:
    • Strengthen the relationship and increase delivered value.
    • Prove that expanding with Dell is worth it.

Paint the Picture of What’s Possible

  • Use:
    • Shannon’s asset views (MyService360 / AIOps).
    • What you saw on walk-throughs and in clinical / campus workflows.
    • Early wins and mentor examples.
  • For key accounts, build a simple Now / Next / Later roadmap to present in QBRs:
Now
  • Stabilize, secure, and tune the installed base.
  • Resolve support/performance issues and close EOS gaps.
  • Lock in critical renewals and immediate risk mitigation.
Next
  • Targeted modernization: backup, storage, VDI, standard clinic/hospital stack, or core campus services.
  • Launch 1–2 focused pilots that align to their roadmap.
  • Extend services to reduce operational burden.
Later
  • More AI- and data-driven operations and use cases.
  • Scaling AI-ready infrastructure and advanced analytics.
  • Deeper automation of operations and resilience.

Lock in Plans to Maintain & Grow Spend (by Day 90)

  • For each current spender, have:
    • A clear view of installed base, services, risk points, and likely needs for 12–24 months.
    • A concrete plan to keep them spending:
      • Renewals and refresh cycles.
      • 1–2 expansion ideas tied directly to their roadmap.
    • A planning conversation about avoiding surprises and the value of ongoing evaluation with Dell.

Partner Strategy

  • Where partners matter, align on joint plans, roles, and engagement model.
  • Present one clear Dell + partner story to the customer.

Show Resilience & Progress

  • Continue prospecting even while working active deals.
  • When something slips, capture the lesson and adjust approach.

By Day 90, Be Able to Show:

  • Activity trend – activity level over the first 90 days and how it has changed.
  • Pipeline coverage – coverage versus target, by account and tower.
  • Anchor wins – one or two anchor wins or near-term deals with clear value stories.
  • Account roadmaps – evidence that you are thinking beyond one-off transactions toward multi-year value and partnership.
